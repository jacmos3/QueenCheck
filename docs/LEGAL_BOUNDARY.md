# Product and legal boundary

This document records engineering constraints. It is not legal advice or a regulatory opinion.

QueenCheck deliberately omits the former project's token, staking, bonding, rewards, prize pools, wagering, arbitration deposits, token governance, treasury, and emissions. The optional game record is non-transferable, opt-in, burnable, and has no promised financial, redemption, access, revenue, voting, or reward right.

These properties reduce several obvious financial-regulation and consumer-risk vectors; they do not make every product use automatically lawful. In particular, paid entry, promotional prize competitions, sponsored tournaments, secondary wrappers, marketing claims of value, personal-data processing, sanctions exposure, and jurisdiction-specific gaming rules require separate review before launch.

The software and website must not describe the record as an investment, asset with appreciation potential, yield source, reward entitlement, or transferable collectible. Third parties can fork MIT-licensed code, but their changes and claims are not QueenCheck releases.
